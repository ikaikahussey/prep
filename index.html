Neighbor Check-In

We're asking volunteers to check on four nearby households before and after a storm. This page collects volunteer sign-ups and lists the emergency kit contents recommended by the Hawaiʻi Emergency Management Agency.

What volunteers do

  1. Choose four households within walking distance. Priority: residents living alone, kūpuna, residents without a vehicle, households with infants.
  2. When a watch or warning is issued, confirm each household has water, food, medication, and a working alert source.
  3. After conditions clear, confirm each household is accounted for. Report medical emergencies to 911.

This is not an alert channel and does not replace official warnings. Emergencies: call 911.
